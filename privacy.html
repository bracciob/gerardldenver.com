Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Gerard Langhuoth, REALTOR®, doing business as Finish Line Properties at Guide Real Estate, 720 S Colorado Blvd, Suite 215A, Glendale, CO 80246. You can reach us at (720) 312-1032 or Gerard.finishline@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us through our inquiry form — typically your name, email address, phone number, and the details of your message. We also receive limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type, and the pages you visit, which helps us keep the site working properly.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, and we do not collect sensitive personal data such as government ID numbers or financial account details through this website.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, to provide real estate brokerage services you request, and to follow up about properties or services that match what you told us you were looking for. We may also use it to comply with legal, licensing, and recordkeeping obligations that apply to Colorado real estate professionals.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

When we share information

We share information only when it is necessary: with our brokerage, Guide Real Estate; with service providers who help us operate the site, host it, or send email on our behalf; with transaction partners such as title companies, lenders, or inspectors when you ask us to; and when required by law or to protect legal rights.

Property search and listing data

Listing information shown on this site is provided for your personal, non-commercial use and is believed to be reliable but is not guaranteed. Availability, pricing, and property details change frequently and should be independently verified.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses only the cookies and local browser storage needed for basic functionality — for example, remembering that you have already seen the site's intro animation during your visit. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to serve you and to satisfy the recordkeeping requirements that apply to real estate transactions, then delete it.

Your privacy rights (including Colorado)

Under the Colorado Privacy Act and similar state laws, you may have the right to confirm whether we process your personal data, access a copy of it, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, obtain a portable copy, and opt out of the sale of personal data or targeted advertising (we do neither).

To exercise any of these rights, email Gerard.finishline@gmail.com or call (720) 312-1032. We will verify your request and respond within the timeframe the law allows. If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our response.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, and we take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive financial details through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version takes effect when posted here, and the date above will change to reflect it.
