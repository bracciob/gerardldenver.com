Meet Gerard

A Denver native with a global perspective.

Raised in Denver, Gerard Langhuoth brings deep, firsthand knowledge of the city's many charming neighborhoods to every client he serves. Integrity, honor, business acumen, and marketing expertise are the values he loves to share.

Before real estate, Gerard held a regional sales manager role with a national distribution company — experience that sharpened his negotiation instincts and gave him familiarity with markets from the Front Range to San Francisco and Fort Lauderdale.
